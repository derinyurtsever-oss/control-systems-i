How to run this
  1. The three snapshots first. Press 1, 2, 3 for K = 1, 10, 20 — the three little plots at the top of page 8. Each leaves an orange pair behind. Ask the room where the poles will be at K = 5 before you go further.
  2. Then start over and sweep. The unstable pole at +1 walks left and reaches 0 at K = 10: exactly on the edge. Just after that both poles are in the left half-plane.
    "A single number in front of the plant, and the system is stable."
  3. The breakaway. At K = 11.25 the two poles meet at −0.5 and leave the real axis. From then on the real part never changes — more gain only makes it oscillate faster.
  4. Show the rules. Start at the poles, real axis between them (odd number of poles to the right), two poles and no zeros so two asymptotes at ±90° through the centroid (−2 + 1)/2 = −0.5. That is the whole locus, without solving anything. Best saved until after the students have tried the first example on page 9 themselves.
