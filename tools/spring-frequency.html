How to run this
  1. The three experiments from page 3. Press 1, 2, 3 in turn. Before each one, ask the room how far the mass will swing compared to the hand. Let the start-up die out (the grey bar), then read off the two numbers.
    "Same frequency as the hand, every time. Only the size and the delay change."
  2. ω₂ is the surprise. The hand moves at the spring's own speed: the mass swings 2.5× as far, exactly a quarter period late. ω₃: it barely moves and goes the opposite way to the hand (half a period late).
  3. One complex number (page 4). Point at the arrows: the blue arrow is always the red one, stretched and turned back by the same angle. What you see on the plot is just their shadows on the real axis.
  4. Every speed at once. Each experiment you finish drops a dot. Then press G: the curve is |G(jω)| and ∠G(jω), computed without solving the ODE once. Drag the slider along it — the peak near ω = 1 is resonance.

m = k = 1, c = 0.4. The mass starts hanging still under the hand. Time runs 2.5× faster than real time.
