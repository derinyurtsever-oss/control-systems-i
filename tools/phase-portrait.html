How to run this
  1. ① ② ③ from page 7. Spiral in, spiral out, circle. Drag the pair slowly from left to right across the imaginary axis and say what you see: the real part decides in or out, the imaginary part decides how much it turns.
    "Left half-plane, it comes home. Right half-plane, it leaves. On the axis, it goes round forever."
  2. Pull the pair down onto the real axis. The spiral stops turning and becomes a node. Drag one × to the right of zero and it is a saddle — one way in, one way out. That is the balanced pendulum (7).
  3. The exception. Press 3 for the circle, drag both onto the origin, then press J. Same eigenvalue 0 twice, but now A = [0 1; 0 0] and the trajectories drift away along straight lines: the t·e0t = t term. Press J again: without the Jordan block nothing moves at all — Lyapunov stable.
  4. Guess the eigenvalues (page 8). Click a preset with the eigenvalue card covered, have the room guess, then reveal.
  5. Your turn (page 10). Type the worksheet's matrices into A after the students have had a go, and follow the tree.
