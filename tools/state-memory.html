Three cups, three completely different mornings. Watch what they have in common.

drink 50 ml/min
How to run this
  1. Play the three pasts. Three different mornings — pours, sips, a spill. Let them run and point out how little they have in common.
    "Nothing about these three mornings is the same."
  2. It stops at "now". All three cups hold 200 ml. Ask what you would need to know to predict the next ten minutes — the whole story, or just the number in the cup?
  3. Press space again. The same input goes to all three, and the three curves become one.
    "The future needed one number, not the history. That number is the state."
    Change the input with the slider and it still holds.
