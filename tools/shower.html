How to run this
  1. Patient (1). You step in, it's a bit cold, you turn the tap a little. Hot, cold, hot — but every swing is smaller. Press T: the toilet flush knocks it cold, and it calms down again.
  2. Impatient (3). Same water, same pipe — only the hand is different. Every swing is bigger than the last.
    "The plant alone is harmless. The closed loop with us in it makes it oscillate."
  3. The edge (2). K = 8: the swings neither grow nor shrink. One trip around the loop gives back exactly what went in: L(jω) = −1. On the Nyquist plot the curve passes straight through the red point.
  4. Back to page 7. This is the same L(s) = K/(s + 1)³ as the example: 4, 8, 16. Each time the curve's crossing goes past −1, it wraps around it twice — and the root locus gets two poles in the right half-plane.
