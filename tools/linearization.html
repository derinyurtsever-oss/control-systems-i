How to run this
  1. 5°, then 15°. Let go: the two pendulums swing as one. Point at the zoom panel — the green curve and the red tangent are on top of each other here.
    "Zoom in far enough and any smooth curve is a straight line."
  2. 30°, then 90°. The orange model pulls ahead, because it thinks the swing is faster than it is. In the zoom panel the dots split apart: that gap is exactly what linearization threw away.
    "Leave the neighbourhood and the linear model starts telling stories."
  3. Linearize at the top. The other equilibrium. Both start balanced 5° off vertical. For a moment they agree — then the real one falls and swings, while the model says the angle grows forever.
    "Which point you linearize around is the first question, not a detail."

What was thrown away is second order, so halving the swing quarters the error: 30° is off by 4.7%, 15° by 1.2%. Keys: space let go / pause · R reset · 1–5 presets · T top or bottom · ←/→ nudge the angle.
