How to run this
  1. The three arrows first. Press 1, 2, 3 for ω = 0.2, 1 and 5 — the green, orange and purple arrows from the sheet. Each one drops a dot on the Bode plot. Before each one, ask: longer or shorter, steeper or flatter?
  2. Then start over and sweep. At the bottom the arrow barely moves: flat at 0 dB. At ω = 1 it is the diagonal of a unit square: √2 long, 45°. Above that it is basically just jω.
    "Ten times higher, ten times longer arrow, a tenth of the gain: −20 dB per decade."
  3. Straight lines. Press A for the asymptotes: flat, then −20 dB/decade from the corner at ω = 1; the phase ramps from 0° to −90° between 0.1 and 10. The real curve is never more than 3 dB away.
  4. Then the rest of the alphabet. A zero is the same arrow in the numerator, so everything flips: +20 dB/decade and +90°.
